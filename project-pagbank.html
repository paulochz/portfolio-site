Context and business challenge

PagBank is a digital banking platform serving millions of customers in Brazil. One recurring operational issue was the balance unlock process. When accounts were temporarily blocked due to compliance or verification requirements, customers needed to submit documents for review.

The existing experience was fragmented across channels, unclear in its instructions, and highly dependent on customer support. This created friction for users and increased operational costs for the business.

The goal was to redesign the entire balance unlock journey, reducing support demand, improving first-time document submission rates, and increasing user confidence throughout the process.

The problem

Customers faced significant friction at every stage of the balance unlock journey:
  • Difficulty understanding why their balance was blocked
  • No clear indication of where to submit required documents
  • Conflicting information across help center, email, and platform
  • Frustration with image size limits and document formatting
  • No visibility into analysis status after submission
  • Inability to resume the process after interruption
  • From a business perspective, this resulted in a high volume of support tickets, repeated submissions due to errors, and longer processing times for both users and internal teams.

Operations Blueprint & User Flow

To gain a holistic understanding of the flow, a blueprint was created connecting every interaction from the operator-managed analysis to the final user.

Hypothesis

Based on research findings, we defined a set of hypotheses to guide the redesign:

Increasing visibility and clarity of the submission entry point would reduce help center dependency

Increasing visibility of the process status for the user through notifications, emails, and status tracking will reduce the user's anxiety, causing them to avoid contacting other channels, such as the help center.

Implementing a way to compress the image after upload or increasing the maximum size allowed will take this responsibility off the user and reduce stress when submitting the document.

Providing an in-app flow within the mobile application would assist those without a computer in submitting documents more easily.

Displaying information about the deadline for completing the process directly in the flow will allow the user to be more informed about the progress and avoid seeking human assistance for visibility.

When many documents are requested, the user may delay submission after starting the process. When returning to submit, the user may be frustrated by not being able to continue where they left off.

Web Interface

The web interface is designed to be intuitive and user-friendly, ensuring a seamless experience for users accessing the balance unlock process through their web browser.

Insights

Improved User Experience

To improve our workflow, we need to take action before the submission. This means providing advance information to the user and educating them about the submission process. By making it clearer to users what is expected, we will be able to identify areas for improvement.

Better Understanding of Analysis

In a client-centric point of view: they will have a better understanding of the analysis and its implications. This will lead to more informed decisions and a clearer grasp of the next steps they need to take. As a result, users can feel more confident and prepared, knowing exactly what to expect from the analysis.

Reduced Operational Confusion

In an operational point of view: the team was able to map out the user experience by creating a logical and efficient structure. This will reduce confusion and improve system efficiency.

Conclusion

The PagBank balance unlock process redesign was a significant improvement in user experience, reducing customer support tickets and increasing user satisfaction. By focusing on user needs and providing clear, intuitive steps, we were able to create a seamless and efficient process that benefits both users and the company.
